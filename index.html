HOW TO PLAY

New hire orientation · Mandatory · Probably

The job

Pick an order (1 is the tutorial, 5 is the whole nightmare) and ship it as fast as you can. Every incident adds 15 seconds to your time. Beat your best, and go for gold, silver or bronze.

Who's who

Ralph drives the forklift. He has a cast on his leg and his crutches in the cab, so he never gets off the truck. He has the pick list, too: it's the clipboard in his cab.

Dave is on foot. He walks, climbs, picks boxes off shelves, carries them, and punts them.

Single player: swap between them any time with Tab / View. Couch co-op: split screen, one player each.

The order, step by step

  1. Read the pick list on Ralph's clipboard: two SKUs, how many of each, and where they live. A location like C-4-2 is rack C, bay 4 (counting from the west end), shelf 2 (counting from the bottom).
  2. Find the boxes. Racks A to G are on the main floor. Every box has its SKU on all four sides, and neighbours can look alike, so check the number.
  3. Short? An orange BACKSTOCK sign under a shelf points to more of that SKU on racks H and I, up on the mezzanine. Not every sign is for your box.
  4. Build one pallet with the full quantity of both SKUs on it.
  5. Wrap it. Set the pallet in the WRAP square under the stretch wrapper (by the ramp), back the forklift out, and leave it still for a moment.
  6. Ship it. Drive the wrapped pallet down the ramp to the open dock in the basement (door up, green lamp; the clipboard says which) and into the trailer. Forks below 1 m, or the mast won't fit through the door.

Reaching the shelves

Shelf 1 is in reach from the floor. Shelf 2 needs a ladder or the forks. Shelf 3 needs the forks raised high, or a ladder that Ralph lifts up on the forks with Dave on it.

Riding the forks: Dave stands on the blades or a pallet and Ralph lifts him. Press E / X to hold on: then nothing Ralph does can throw him off, but he faces straight out and can't look round. From the forks he reaches a little further, and E on a box takes it (he lets go to grab it). He can even catch the forks mid-jump.

Ladders hang on LADDERS stands in the basement and on the mezzanine landing. E shoulders one, E sets it up where the ghost turns green, then walk into the steps and climb with W / S. From the top step Dave reaches shelf 2. Jumping off works, but it always knocks the ladder over.

The mezzanine (racks H and I): take the stairs at its east end, or ride a pallet up on the forks to the PALLET DROP gap in its railing.

Moving boxes

Pick up with E: aim at the bottom box of a stack to take up to three. E again puts them down where the ghost shows.

Punt with R / RB: hold to aim, and the ghost shows exactly where the box will land; aim higher to punt further. Let go and Dave boots it. Great for landing boxes on a pallet, or on raised forks.

Conveyors carry anything on them north and drop it on the pallet at the end. The packing benches' tall back panels make a backboard: punt a box through a gap between benches at the far panel and it drops onto the packing line.

FRAGILE boxes explode. Punt one, drop one, or get knocked flat holding one, and most of the warehouse goes up with it. Set them down gently.

Incidents

Every incident stops the shift for a CCTV replay and a Form 300 incident report, and counts against you. Classics include:

  • Falling more than about 2.5 m, or riding a ladder over.
  • Racks toppling: a fast forklift hit sends them over, and they take their neighbours down like dominoes.
  • Getting hit by the forklift, or having the forks slammed into the floor while riding them (broken legs).
  • Tipping the forklift. It explodes. Ralph is somehow fine.
  • Standing in the wrapper while it wraps.
  • FRAGILE boxes, see above.

Hazards

  • A section of the basement's railing is missing, right beside the wrapper. The hazard tape stops nothing.
  • The electrical panel on the east wall sparks as it drips, and the outlet behind the ramp crackles.
  • Mystery barrels by the south wall leak and gush when knocked over (don't ask what's in them).
  • Every FRAGILE box is a bomb, and racks topple like dominoes.

When it all goes wrong

  • Hold E / X for 3 s near knocked-over racking to put every rack and its stock back.
  • Hold Q / Y for 1 s to send your character back to the start (the forklift comes back upright).
  • Hold E / X near a tipped pallet to flip it flat, or R / RB near a fallen ladder to stand it up.

Tips

  • Ralph reads the pick list out. In single player, swap to Ralph to check it.
  • Park a pallet on the forks and punt boxes straight onto it.
  • Drive with the forks low. Raised forks make the truck tippy.
