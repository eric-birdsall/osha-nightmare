HOW TO PLAY

New hire orientation · Mandatory · Probably

Receiving / putaway

Choose Receiving for five purchase orders: the tutorial, higher shelves, mixed pallets, high storage, and a combined delivery. Ralph unloads shrinkwrapped pallets from the dock listed on the cab clipboard. Dave holds EX for one second to cut wrap—even while a pallet is on the forks—then places individual cartons in their listed low-shelf locations. Lines marked WRAPPED PALLET must stay wrapped and be seated in the correct high bay. The stretch wrapper accepts partially full stock pallets too. Only the tutorial has yellow PUTAWAY signs, destination arrows and instructions; all POs keep clipboard progress. Wrong locations remain unfinished. Receiving finishes when all required stock is correctly put away; cleanup and parking are optional.

Wrapped reserve stock

High-storage pallets start wrapped in picking too. Ralph brings one down, Dave cuts the wrap and picks the required cartons. Rewrap any leftovers for secure transport using the existing stretch wrapper. Returning reserve stock and cleaning up are optional.

The picking job

Pick an order (1 is the tutorial, 5 is the whole nightmare) and ship it as fast as you can, or take on a Full Shift: all five back to back, mess and all, on one clock. Every incident adds 15 seconds to your time. Beat your best, and go for gold, silver or bronze.

Who's who

Ralph drives the forklift. He has a cast on his leg and his crutches in the cab, so he never gets off the truck. He has the pick list, too: it's the clipboard in his cab.

Dave is on foot. He walks, climbs, picks boxes off shelves, carries them, and punts them.

Single player: swap between them any time with TabView. Couch co-op: split screen, one player each.

The order, step by step

  1. Read the pick list on Ralph's clipboard: two SKUs, how many of each, and where they live. A location like C-4-2 is rack C, bay 4 (counting from the west end), shelf 2 (counting from the bottom).
  2. Find the boxes. Racks A to G are on the main floor. Every box has its SKU on all four sides, and neighbours can look alike, so check the number.
  3. Short? An orange BACKSTOCK sign under a shelf points to more of that SKU on racks H and I, up on the mezzanine. Not every sign is for your box.
  4. Build one pallet with the full quantity of both SKUs on it.
  5. Wrap it. Set the pallet in the WRAP square under the stretch wrapper (by the ramp), back the forklift out, and leave it still for a moment.
  6. Ship it. Drive the wrapped pallet down the ramp to the open dock in the basement (door up, green lamp; the clipboard says which) and into the trailer. Forks below 1 m, or the mast won't fit through the door.

Reaching the shelves

Shelf 1 is in reach from the floor. Shelf 2 needs a ladder or the forks. Shelf 3 needs the forks raised high, or a ladder that Ralph lifts up on the forks with Dave on it.

Riding the forks: Dave stands on the blades or a pallet and Ralph lifts him. Press EX to hold on: then nothing Ralph does can throw him off, but he faces straight out and can't look round. From the forks he reaches a little further, and EX on a box takes it (he lets go to grab it). He can even catch the forks mid-jump.

Ladders hang on stands by the basement railing on the main floor and beside Rack I on the mezzanine. EX shoulders one, EX sets it up where the ghost turns green, then walk into the steps and climb with W / Sleft stick. From the top step Dave reaches shelf 2. Jumping off works, but it always knocks the ladder over. Dave can also jump over railings, but he can't grab a box mid-jump.

The mezzanine (racks H and I): take the stairs at its east end, or ride a pallet up on the forks to the PALLET DROP gap in its railing.

Moving boxes

Pick up with EX: aim at the bottom box of a stack to take up to three. Tap EX again to put them down where the ghost shows.

Punt by holding EX with a box in hand: hold to aim, and the ghost shows exactly where the box will land. Looking straight ahead lobs it well up and out; look down to keep it short. Let go and Dave boots it. Great for landing boxes on a pallet, or on raised forks.

Conveyors carry anything on them north and drop it on the pallet at the end. The packing benches' tall back panels make a backboard: punt a box through a gap between benches at the far panel and it drops onto the packing line.

FRAGILE boxes explode. Punt one, drop one, or get knocked flat holding one, and most of the warehouse goes up with it. Set them down gently.

Incidents

Every incident adds 15 seconds to your time (the clock flashes red). With CCTV Replays on (Controls screen), the security cameras then show you what happened, with the clock stopped while it plays; any button skips it. Classics include:

  • Falling more than about 2.5 m, or riding a ladder over.
  • Racks toppling: a fast forklift hit sends them over, and they take their neighbours down like dominoes.
  • Getting hit by the forklift, or having the forks slammed into the floor while riding them (broken legs).
  • Tipping the forklift. Ralph can hold his Back to start control to reset it upright in parking.
  • Standing in the wrapper while it wraps.
  • FRAGILE boxes, see above.

Hazards

  • A section of the basement's railing is missing, right beside the wrapper. The hazard tape stops nothing.
  • The electrical panel on the east wall sparks as it drips.
  • Every FRAGILE box is a bomb, and racks topple like dominoes.

When it all goes wrong

  • Hold RRB for 3 s near knocked-over racking to put every rack and its stock back.
  • Hold QY for 1 s to send your character back to the start.
  • Hold EX near a tipped pallet or wrapped load to flip it upright, or RRB near a fallen ladder to stand it up.
  • Forklift on its side? Ralph holds QY for 1 s to reset upright in parking. Dave cannot reset the truck.

Tips

  • Ralph reads the pick list out. In single player, swap to Ralph to check it.
  • Park a pallet on the forks and punt boxes straight onto it.
  • Drive with the forks low. Raised forks make the truck tippy.
